This file is for documenting important math and physics concepts. 

=== Important Constants === 
Gravitational Constant G = 6.674e-11 N * m^2 / kg^2

=== Gravity === 

Newton's Law of gravity: 
F = G m_1 * m_2 / r^2 

The force of gravity is equivalent to the product of the masses of the two bodies, 
divided by the sqaure of the distance from their center of masses. (G is a constant
factor, namely the Gravitational constant)


=== Velocities === 

Orbital Velocity 
v = sqrt { G * m / r } 
The oribital velocity is the square root of the mass of the body over the distance
of the object from the center of the body. Does not depend on the mass of the object. 

=== Shapes === 

Parabolas 
Often these are failed orbits, when the object leaving a body fails to 
enter orbit and crashes.

Elipses 
A defined orbit, a perfect one is a circle.

Hyperbola
The expanded elipse, a model of escape trajectory.
